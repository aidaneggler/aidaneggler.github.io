Two Unity course projects, from puzzles to dungeon combat.

01Unity · C# · 3D

Spectrum

First-person puzzle game

An escape-room game about seeing things in a different light. Switch between white, UV, and red flashlights to uncover clues and find your way out.

Play in browser ↗
About the project

Collect light sources, reveal hidden information, and connect clues across a series of room puzzles. The game combines first-person exploration with objects that respond to different wavelengths of light.

  • White, UV, and red flashlight switching
  • Hidden clues, a keypad, and interactive objects
  • A two-level puzzle progression

Team course project

02Unity · C# · 2D

Dungeon recreation

Zelda-inspired adventure

A recreation of a classic dungeon adventure. Explore connected rooms, fight enemies, and collect the items you need to keep moving.

Play in browser ↗
About the project

A course project exploring the systems behind the original Legend of Zelda’s first dungeon: room transitions, sword combat, enemy behavior, items, and a heart-based health system.

  • Sword combat, enemy damage, and knockback
  • Hearts, item pickups, and room transitions
  • Classic top-down dungeon exploration

Team course project · Based on The Legend of Zelda (Nintendo)
